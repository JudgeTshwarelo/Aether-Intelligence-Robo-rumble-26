Smart Wearable — Complete CAD Design Brief

Design a real, functional, 3D-printable smartwatch enclosure for the Smart Wearable project. The watch should look like a polished, modern smartwatch while providing enough internal space for the electronics.

The design must be openable, rechargeable, serviceable, and practical to assemble.

1. Overall form

- Approximate watch diameter: 45 mm
- Keep the watch as compact and slim as reasonably possible.
- Target overall thickness: approximately 10–12 mm, depending on the actual component dimensions.
- Use a smooth, rounded, modern smartwatch shape rather than a bulky electronics box.
- Rounded edges and comfortable surfaces should make it look wearable.
- Use the AETHER WATCH V1 engineering sheet as the dimensional baseline where applicable.
- The final design should look like a real consumer wearable, not a rough prototype enclosure.

2. Two-piece / openable construction

The watch must consist of an openable enclosure, with a main body and removable cover.

The enclosure must allow the user to open the watch to:

- Access the electronics
- Replace/service the battery
- Inspect wiring
- Repair or replace components
- Maintain the device

Use suitable screw posts, fastening points, clips, or another practical fastening method. Avoid permanently sealing the electronics inside.

The cover should fit securely without making the watch difficult to open.

3. Internal electronics cavity

The inside must have enough room for the actual components, including:

- Mini ESP32 development board
- OLED display
- Rechargeable battery
- Heart-rate sensor
- Capacitive/skin-contact sensor
- GPS/location module
- GSM/communication module
- Buzzer
- LEDs
- Buttons
- Wiring and connectors

Do not design the cavity to exactly match the component dimensions. Include reasonable clearance for wiring, connectors, assembly and tolerances.

Include internal mounting supports/standoffs where useful so components do not move freely inside the watch.

4. OLED display

The OLED is a major part of the front design.

Create a clean OLED viewing window/opening on the top cover.

The OLED should be:

- Centrally positioned
- Properly supported from underneath
- Protected by the surrounding case
- Easy to see from the front
- Integrated into the design rather than simply sitting on top of the case

The front geometry should be designed around the actual OLED dimensions.

5. SOS button and controls

Provide a clearly accessible SOS button on the side of the watch.

The button should:

- Be easy to press
- Not interfere with the wrist
- Have enough clearance for its physical mechanism
- Be integrated into the overall aesthetic

Provide additional openings/mounting locations for other required controls where necessary.

6. LEDs

Provide suitable positions for the status LEDs.

The LEDs should be visible without making the watch look cluttered.

Use small openings/light guides where appropriate.

7. Buzzer / sound

Provide a suitable sound outlet/grille for the buzzer.

The buzzer should remain protected inside the enclosure while allowing the alarm sound to escape.

Use a neat pattern of small holes rather than one large exposed opening.

8. Rear / skin-contact area

The rear of the watch must include the required skin-contact sensor area.

This is important:

Do not completely seal the sensor behind solid plastic.

Provide an appropriate:

- Sensor opening/window, or
- Raised sensor-contact area

so that the sensor can physically reach the user's skin when the watch is worn.

The rear should remain comfortable against the wrist.

9. Rechargeable battery

The watch must be designed around a rechargeable battery.

Provide:

- A dedicated battery area
- Enough clearance around the battery
- A secure method of holding the battery in place
- Safe separation from other components where practical
- Space for the battery wiring/connector

The enclosure should also accommodate the selected charging method/charging connector.

The charging port should be accessible without opening the entire watch.

10. GPS and communication modules

Provide internal space for the location and communication hardware.

The design should avoid completely surrounding these modules with unnecessary material where that could interfere with their intended operation.

Provide reasonable clearance and positioning for:

- GPS/location module and antenna
- GSM/communication module and antenna

The exact internal arrangement should be adjusted according to the actual component dimensions.

11. Strap attachment

Add strong, clean watch-strap mounting points/lugs.

The strap attachment should:

- Be strong enough for normal wrist use
- Look integrated into the case
- Avoid sharp edges
- Allow a practical watch strap to be attached

12. Manufacturing requirements

The enclosure should be designed for 3D printing.

Avoid unnecessary:

- Extremely thin walls
- Unsupported overhangs
- Tiny features that cannot print reliably
- Fragile attachment points

Use practical wall thicknesses and tolerances suitable for a student-level FDM 3D printer.

The final design should be possible to print as separate parts and assemble afterward.

13. Design priorities

The priorities are:

1. Functional
2. Openable and serviceable
3. Rechargeable
4. Compact
5. Comfortable to wear
6. Easy to 3D-print
7. Clean and professional appearance

The final Smart Wearable should look like a real smartwatch, not an electronics project box.

14. Final CAD output

Create the design as separate, clearly identifiable components:

- Main/bottom watch body
- Removable top cover
- OLED opening/window
- Internal electronics cavity
- Battery compartment/clearance
- Component mounting supports
- SOS button opening
- Other control openings
- LED openings/light guides
- Buzzer/speaker openings
- Rear skin-contact sensor opening/contact area
- Charging-port opening
- GPS/communication clearance
- Strap attachment points

The parts must fit together correctly and remain openable after assembly.

The final goal is a compact, sleek, functional, rechargeable and serviceable Smart Wearable watch enclosure that can be 3D-printed and used as the physical housing for the actual electronics.
