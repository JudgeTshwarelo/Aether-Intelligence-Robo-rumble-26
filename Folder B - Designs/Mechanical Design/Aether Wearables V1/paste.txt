Design a complete 3D-printable rugged handheld emergency communication device called:

AETHER WATCH V1
Adaptive Health & Emergency Assistant

Use the attached Garmin inReach Mini 3 image as the primary industrial-design reference for the overall silhouette, proportions, ruggedness, and antenna placement, BUT DO NOT COPY THE GARMIN LOGO, branding, exact geometry, or proprietary design.

The device should look like a professional field-deployed emergency communication device designed by an engineering team for rural emergency response, disaster response, medical teams, firefighters, police, rescue teams, hikers, and remote communities.

IMPORTANT:
This is a physical prototype enclosure intended for 3D printing. Prioritize manufacturability, realistic component placement, internal clearances, wall thickness, cable routing, assembly, and serviceability over purely cosmetic appearance.

==================================================
1. OVERALL DEVICE
==================================================

Create a compact rugged rectangular handheld device with rounded corners and a thick protective enclosure.

Overall external dimensions:

WIDTH: 50 mm
HEIGHT: 68 mm
DEPTH: 22 mm

The device should have:
- rounded outer corners
- ruggedized side bumpers
- slightly raised protective bezel around the display
- thick impact-resistant enclosure
- professional industrial-design appearance
- compact proportions similar to a satellite communicator
- ergonomic shape that can be held in one hand
- clearly defined front, rear, top, left, right, and bottom surfaces

Use approximately 2 mm minimum enclosure wall thickness.

Use larger radii on the external corners, approximately 4–6 mm, to create a rugged handheld appearance.

The enclosure consists of:
1. Main front housing
2. Rear removable cover
3. Internal component mounting system
4. Top antenna housing
5. Protective side structures
6. Optional strap/lanyard mounting points

==================================================
2. FRONT FACE
==================================================

The front face contains the OLED display.

Use a 0.96-inch I2C OLED module.

Approximate OLED PCB dimensions:

27 mm × 27 mm

Approximate visible display area:

23 mm × 13 mm

Create a recessed OLED mounting pocket approximately:

27 mm × 27 mm

The OLED must sit inside the enclosure rather than simply being placed on top.

Create a smaller front viewing window approximately:

23 mm × 13 mm

The OLED viewing window should expose only the actual display area while hiding the PCB.

Create a raised protective bezel around the display.

The bezel should protrude approximately 1–1.5 mm from the front surface.

The OLED should be positioned slightly above the vertical center of the device.

Leave sufficient space below the display for status indicators and emergency controls.

The front face should look like a professional field communicator, not a smartwatch.

==================================================
3. EMERGENCY / SOS BUTTON
==================================================

Create a dedicated physical emergency button.

Button type:

6 mm × 6 mm tactile push button.

Create an external button opening approximately:

7 mm diameter

Position the emergency button on the RIGHT SIDE of the device.

The button should be positioned approximately around the upper-middle portion of the enclosure.

The button should be:
- easy to locate without looking
- protected against accidental activation
- slightly recessed or surrounded by a protective guard
- mechanically accessible from outside the enclosure

Add a raised protective ring/guard around the button.

The button should visually communicate:

EMERGENCY / SOS

Use a rugged red physical button in the conceptual visualization.

==================================================
4. STATUS LEDs
==================================================

Create two 5 mm LED mounting positions on the FRONT LOWER portion of the device.

LED holes:

approximately 5.2 mm diameter

Place the LEDs horizontally next to each other.

Spacing:

approximately 10–14 mm center-to-center.

LED 1:
GREEN
System / communication status

LED 2:
RED
Emergency / alert status

The LEDs should sit beneath the OLED.

Create small recessed circular pockets around the LEDs so they are protected from impacts.

The LED lenses should remain visible from the front.

==================================================
5. BUZZER / AUDIO OUTPUT
==================================================

Place the buzzer on the REAR of the enclosure.

Use approximately:

12 mm diameter buzzer

Do not create one huge exposed opening.

Instead create a professional acoustic grille consisting of multiple small circular holes.

Recommended:

6–8 holes

Each hole:

approximately 2 mm diameter

Arrange the holes symmetrically in a circular or hexagonal pattern.

The grille should be recessed slightly into the rear housing.

The design should protect the buzzer from direct impact while allowing sound to escape.

==================================================
6. ESP32-C3 INTERNAL MOUNT
==================================================

The primary controller is an ESP32-C3 development board.

Approximate board dimensions:

52 mm × 28 mm × 12 mm

Create an internal mounting area specifically for the ESP32-C3.

Do NOT permanently fuse the ESP32 into the enclosure.

Create mechanical supports/standoffs that allow the board to be secured.

Use four mounting supports where physically appropriate.

Suggested standoff dimensions:

4 mm × 4 mm

approximately 5–6 mm tall.

Include clearance around:
- USB connector
- GPIO pins
- antenna area
- wiring
- reset/boot controls

The ESP32 should be removable for maintenance.

The USB connector must remain accessible.

==================================================
7. BATTERY COMPARTMENT
==================================================

The power source is a 3.7 V LiPo battery.

Approximate battery dimensions:

40 mm × 30 mm × 6 mm

Create a dedicated battery compartment inside the enclosure.

The battery must NOT interfere with:
- ESP32
- OLED PCB
- buzzer
- button
- wiring
- rear cover

Create a shallow battery pocket with approximately:

0.5–1 mm additional clearance around the battery.

Do not compress the battery.

Create a removable or serviceable arrangement so the battery can be replaced.

Provide a protected cable channel from the battery toward the ESP32 power connection.

Avoid sharp internal edges that could damage battery wires.

==================================================
8. USB-C / CHARGING PORT
==================================================

Create a USB-C connector opening on the BOTTOM edge.

Approximate opening:

10 mm wide
5 mm high

The opening should be centered horizontally.

Create enough internal clearance for the actual USB connector on the ESP32 development board.

Add a small protective lip around the port.

The USB opening should not compromise the enclosure structure.

==================================================
9. TOP ANTENNA HOUSING
==================================================

Create the characteristic vertical antenna structure inspired by rugged satellite communicators.

Position:

TOP CENTER

Create a cylindrical or rounded antenna housing approximately:

16 mm diameter
15 mm height

The antenna housing should merge naturally into the main enclosure.

Use rounded transitions rather than a sharp cylinder-to-body connection.

The antenna housing is initially a mechanical placeholder.

DO NOT assume that the antenna itself is electrically correct.

Design the housing so an actual antenna or RF module can later be installed.

Provide an internal cable-routing path from the antenna region toward the electronics compartment.

==================================================
10. INTERNAL STRUCTURE
==================================================

Show the internal architecture in the CAD design.

The device should contain distinct regions for:

A. OLED
B. ESP32-C3
C. LiPo battery
D. SOS button
E. LEDs
F. buzzer
G. wiring
H. USB connector

Create internal cable channels where possible.

Avoid unnecessary supports that obstruct assembly.

Maintain at least approximately:

1.5–2 mm clearance between enclosure structures and electronics where practical.

Use fillets/chamfers on internal edges.

Do not create impossible-to-print enclosed cavities.

==================================================
11. REAR COVER
==================================================

Create a separate removable rear cover.

Rear cover dimensions should match the main enclosure.

Target thickness:

2 mm

The rear cover should be mechanically removable.

Design either:

A. small screws + bosses

OR

B. snap-fit clips

For the prototype, prefer:

4 small screw bosses

located near the corners.

The screw bosses should not interfere with the ESP32 or battery.

Create approximately 2.5–3 mm screw clearance holes depending on the intended screw size.

The rear cover should have a slight perimeter lip that fits into the main body.

==================================================
12. STRAP / LANYARD MOUNT
==================================================

Add rugged mounting points near the top and bottom.

The device should be compatible with:
- wrist strap
- neck lanyard
- backpack attachment
- belt attachment

Create two reinforced mounting structures.

Do not make them excessively thin.

Minimum structural thickness approximately:

3–4 mm

==================================================

- mounting method
- cable access
- insertion/removal path
- realistic dimensions

The ESP32 must fit inside the internal cavity.

The battery must fit without being compressed.

The USB connector must align with the external opening.

The buzzer must align with the acoustic grille.
